About

Fourteen years behind a viewfinder, most of them spent learning that the technical part is the easy half.

I'm Ali — a photographer and filmmaker who got into this by accident, borrowing my father's old Pentax to shoot a friend's wedding badly. Since then I've worked across editorial, commercial, and documentary work in over twenty countries, always chasing the same thing: a frame that feels inevitable, like it couldn't have been shot any other way. My studio is small on purpose — every project gets my hands on it, from the first location scout to the final grade.
